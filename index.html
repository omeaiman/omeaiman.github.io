Software company (US)Page speed and technical SEO

A 273-page site from 14 seconds to under 3 on a phone

On the company's WordPress site, the main content took a median 14.0 s to appear on a phone, and every page downloaded about 60 stylesheets before it could show anything.

What I did

  • Generated critical CSS for the first screen of every page (534 files, one for phones and one for desktop), each checked against the full page render.
  • Held the remaining stylesheets until the first screen loaded, and made the main image load first, with preloads for the homepage hero and blog featured images.
  • Moved heavy images to WebP: 102 blog featured images went from 167 MB to 32 MB in total, and a 4.1 MB hero became a 50 KB file on phones.
  • Stopped layout jumps with size-matched fallback fonts and per-page styles.
  • Wrote and applied alt text for 237 media items, checked by re-crawling all 274 indexable pages.

How I work

Plan, ship, measure, report

  1. Plan

    A dated plan for each company, with targets agreed before work starts and broken into daily tasks.

  2. Ship

    Fixes and pages backed by the platform's own documentation (Google Search Central, web.dev, schema.org), with a rollback for anything hard to undo. A person approves what goes live.

  3. Measure

    Owned data first: Search Console, GA4 and Bing Webmaster Tools. Semrush for authority and AI visibility, and 20 fixed buyer questions tested in AI assistants.

  4. Report

    Monday KPI reads, Friday summaries and 30, 45 and 60 day checkpoints that say what worked, what did not and what to cut. Every number carries its source and date.
